Getting started with the Teacher Workspace

Welcome

The Teacher Workspace gives you a simplified interface for managing competitions, questions, and tournaments. Use the navigation above to access the different sections.

Quick Guide

  • Dashboard - Your overview with quick stats and actions
  • My Competitions - View and manage your live rounds
  • Create Competition - Set up a new round
  • Question Bank - Browse and manage your questions
  • Create Question - Add a new question to the bank
  • My Tournaments - Manage championships
  • Create Tournament - Set up a new championship
  • Reports - View competition and classroom reports
  • Hall of Fame - See completed tournament standings
