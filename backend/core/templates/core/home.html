Foundation

Staff identities, classrooms and interactive screens are in place. Question authoring, competitions, scoring, tournaments and live competition delivery are delivered in later phases.

  • Real-time communication over WebSocket, for the live rounds.
  • Interactive screens are identified by a registered Screen ID, not by IP address.
  • Screen network addresses are recorded as monitoring data only, so a DHCP change never alters a screen's identity.
